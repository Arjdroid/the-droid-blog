## About Me

Hello there, I'm Arjun 👋

I have a lot of interests, although right now I'm an undergraduate studying Computer Science and Philosophy at the University of California, San Diego. I try to enjoy all that life has to offer, and learn something new every day. I love meeting people, and seeing the great outdoors. I am a sucker for good Sci-Fi, but other genres of literature, movies, and video games are enjoyable too.

## My Life Stack

Smartphone: I've been trying to use my phone less, and mostly succeeded in that endeavour. Still, when I need to drop a message, check my emails, or consult The Almighty Calendar, my experiences are ameliorated by the Pixel 9 Pro. The cameras on it are pretty great too!

Computers: I have a desktop PC at home (Ryzen 7 3700X, RTX 3070), and a Thinkpad T480s, both running Fedora Workstation. My primary computer that's always with me however, is my max-spec M1 MacBook Air. It is still an incredible machine, although I'm starting to push its limits and dread the day it must be replaced.

Coffee: The Hoop for light-roast pour-over black coffee is my new mainstay (I'm sorry AeroPress, you served me well).

Cameras: I carry around the Sony RX-100 II for when I want to take a more meaningful photograph than one I might take with my smartphone. Sometimes I even step up to my Olympus OM-D E-M10 II. Prime lenses are what I prefer: 45mm, and 17mm (90mm and 35mm in Full Frame Equivalent).
